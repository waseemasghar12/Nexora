03 The problem

The frontline economy still runs on paper.

Labor suppliers, staffing firms and 3PL operators manage thousands of workers on Excel, Google Sheets, WhatsApp, paper passports, email approvals and shared folders. At 1,000+ workers, these systems don't scale — they fail.

⚠️

Compliance exposure

Visa and Emirates ID expiries surface at fines and inspections — penalties can reach tens of thousands of AED per violation. A

💸

Payroll leakage

Manual OT and attendance reconciliation drives 2–4% payroll error rates, disputes and WPS breaches. A

🎭

Ghost workers & fraud

No biometric link between the person on site and the person on the payroll file.

📦

Asset leakage

Uniforms, scanners, phones and vehicles issued with no custody chain — 5–10% annual loss is typical. A

⏳

Onboarding drag

5–15 days from hire to deployable through paper flows. Every idle day is lost revenue for a staffing firm.

🕳️

Zero worker history

No portable, verified record of skills, conduct and training. Every employer starts from nothing.

The buyer experiences this as fines, margin leakage, lost contracts and audit fear — a P&L problem, not an "HR problem."

04 Why now

Digital compliance acceleration

WPS enforcement, digital Emirates ID infrastructure and labour-law digitization make manual compliance untenable — and machine-verifiable.

Labor pressure

Labor shortages and thin operator margins force productivity per worker to become a managed metric, not a guess.

AI cost collapse

OCR, face-match and LLM reasoning that cost custom-build millions now cost fils per call — vertical AI is finally unit-economic.

Cloud economics

Regional cloud regions make enterprise-grade, data-resident infrastructure affordable for a seed-stage platform.

Worker mobile adoption

Near-universal smartphone penetration among frontline workers makes GPS check-in, selfie verification and digital payslips deployable at last.

The spreadsheet exodus

Enterprises are actively budgeting to exit spreadsheet operations — procurement is looking for exactly this platform.

11 Defensibility

Six reasons this gets harder to displace every month.

Purpose-built for blue-collar

Workflows, mobile UX and data model designed for deployed workforces — not adapted from office HR.

End-to-end lifecycle

Replacing Nexora means replacing identity, payroll, shifts, assets and client reporting at once. Rip-out cost compounds.

OCR + AI compliance engine

Verified-document and biometric rails tuned to GCC identity systems — years of edge cases baked in.

Assets + payroll + CRM combined

The only place custody, pay and performance meet — the combination is the product, and no adjacent player has all three.

Customer-facing portals

Staffing clients' enterprise customers log into Nexora too — B2B2B lock-in and free distribution in one feature.

GCC-first workflows

WPS, Emirates ID, visa lifecycles and regulator-aware processes — a compliance depth global vendors won't rebuild for one region.

The compounding moat: the Workforce Timeline. As verified work history accrues, workers and employers both gain from staying in-network — a data network effect unique to Nexora.

22 Liquidity trough & levers

We planned the worst quarter before it happens.

Seed capital carries the company through the build phase to Series A at month 14. The trajectory below covers both the plan and a stress case in which the round closes six months late.

Base caseStress: Series A +2 quarters

Base case: an AED 3–4M balance at Series A close. Stress case: a six-month delay bottoms near AED 1M with the first three levers engaged — the balance does not reach zero in either path. Hover for monthly positions.

Five pre-identified levers

Sequenced in advance and triggered on defined cash thresholds. Levers 1–3 require no external party and add ~AED 1.5–2M of headroom A:

1 · Front-load implementation fees — new customers pay setup (AED 15–75K) at signing, pulling cash forward.
2 · Annual-prepay discounts — a modest discount converts 12 months of subscription into cash today.
3 · Defer the second Y2 hiring cohort — one quarter’s delay saves ~AED 0.6M without touching the shipped roadmap.
4 · Accelerate transactional revenue — OCR and payroll rails bill on activity, independent of new logos.
5 · Venture debt facility — AED 3–4M secured against recurring revenue, arranged before it’s needed.

The tightest quarter in the plan is identified, quantified and pre-answered: an AED 3–4M base-case buffer at the Series A close, and ~AED 1M under stress using the first three levers alone.
